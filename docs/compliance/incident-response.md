# Incident response procedure

1. Record the alert, time, system, reporter, and evidence without modifying originals.
2. Triage severity: suspected credential/PII exposure, unauthorized access, availability, integrity, or safety impact.
3. Contain: revoke sessions/keys, isolate affected integration, preserve audit logs, and restrict access.
4. Investigate from immutable logs, deployment history, database backups, and provider records.
5. Notify the owner, privacy contact, affected parties, and authorities when required by the approved legal assessment.
6. Recover from a verified clean release/backup, monitor for recurrence, and document lessons learned.

The security owner must maintain an on-call list, contact tree, notification decision record, tabletop schedule, and incident register.
