# Record of processing activities

| Processing | Data | Purpose/lawful basis | Recipients/processors | Retention decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Account and authentication | Username, email, phone, password hash, profile | Contract/service operation; security obligation | Hosting/database provider, email/SMS provider | Account life plus approved legal/business period |
| Bookings and reviews | Schedule, package, notes, feedback, rating | Contract and service quality | Hosting/database provider | Booking/account retention schedule |
| Payments | Amount, method, references, receipt | Contract, accounting, fraud prevention | Hosting/database, configured payment/OCR providers | Finance/legal schedule |
| Operations | Inventory, supplier and POS records | Legitimate business operation | Hosting/database provider | Operations schedule |
| Security/audit | IP, action, timestamp, metadata | Security and accountability obligation | Hosting/database provider | Immutable security retention schedule |
| Optional communications | Consent status and contact details | Explicit consent | Email provider | Until withdrawal, then suppression record |

International transfers must be documented per provider, destination, contractual safeguard, and review date before production use. The privacy owner must complete the actual provider names, jurisdictions, retention periods, and lawful-basis assessment.
