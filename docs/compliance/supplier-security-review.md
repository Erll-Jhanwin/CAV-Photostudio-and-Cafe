# Supplier/security review

Before enabling a supplier, record its service, data categories, processing location, security/privacy terms, breach notification terms, sub-processors, deletion/return commitment, availability commitment, and owner approval. Re-review annually and after material service or incident changes.

Current integration categories include hosting/database, email, SMS OTP, Google authentication, OCR, payment services, and image/CDN services. The privacy owner must complete supplier names, contracts, transfer safeguards, and renewal dates.
